SHARED FILES - chia se file toc do cao giua cac may
====================================================

Trong goi nay co gi
-------------------
  Cai-dat-Windows.bat    <- Windows: nhan doi vao day
  Cai-dat-macOS.command  <- macOS  : nhan doi vao day
  Cai-dat-Linux.sh       <- Linux  : sh Cai-dat-Linux.sh
  app\                   <- ban than ung dung (co the chay truc tiep, khong can cai)


1. CAI TREN MAY TINH
--------------------
Buoc 1: Cai Node.js ban LTS o https://nodejs.org (chi lam 1 lan cho ca may).
Buoc 2: Nhan doi file cai dat tuong ung voi he dieu hanh cua may.

  - Windows: tao san loi tat "Shared Files" ngoai Desktop va trong Start Menu.
  - macOS  : tao app trong thu muc Applications cua Home.
             Neu bao "khong mo duoc vi khong ro nguon goc" -> chuot phai vao
             file cai dat -> Open -> Open.
  - Linux  : tao muc "Shared Files" trong danh sach ung dung.

Khong muon cai: vao thu muc app roi chay start.bat (Windows) /
start.command (macOS) / sh start.sh (Linux).

Mo ung dung se hien mot cua so den in ra dia chi va mot ma QR.
  - Tren chinh may nay          : http://localhost:3000
  - May khac cung wifi          : http://192.168.x.x:3000
Mo dia chi do tren may thu hai la hai may tu thay nhau.
Bam "Chon file" -> bam vao thiet bi kia -> ben kia bam "Nhan". Xong.


2. CAI TREN DIEN THOAI
----------------------
Dien thoai KHONG can file cai dat rieng. App chay tren dien thoai chinh la
trang web cua may tinh, va co the them vao man hinh chinh nhu mot app that.

Cach lam (mat khoang 2 phut, moi dien thoai chi lam 1 lan):

  Buoc 1: Mo ung dung tren may tinh, quet ma QR trong cua so den bang camera
          dien thoai. (Hoac go tay http://192.168.x.x:3000/cai-dat.html)
          Dien thoai phai chung wifi voi may tinh.

  Buoc 2: Trang do se huong dan tai va cai "chung chi" cua may tinh.
          - iPhone : bam nut iPhone/iPad -> vao Cai dat -> Da tai ho so ->
                     Cai dat -> roi vao Cai dat > Cai dat chung > Gioi thieu >
                     Cai dat tin cay chung chi -> bat "Shared Files Local CA".
          - Android: bam nut Android -> vao Cai dat > Bao mat > Cai chung chi
                     > Chung chi CA -> chon file vua tai.

  Buoc 3: Bam nut "Mo ung dung" (dia chi https://...) roi:
          - iPhone : nut Chia se -> "Them vao MH chinh"
          - Android: Chrome se hien "Cai dat ung dung", bam vao

  Xong. App nam o man hinh chinh, mo ra la dung nhu app binh thuong.

Chung chi o buoc 2 la do chinh may tinh cua ban tao ra, khoa rieng nam trong
thu muc .cert tren may tinh va khong gui di dau. No chi de dien thoai khong
bao "khong an toan" khi vao dia chi HTTPS cua may tinh. Neu bo qua buoc nay
thi van dung duoc, chi la file nhan ve phai di qua bo nho tam cua trinh duyet
(file vai GB se nang may) va khong them duoc app vao man hinh chinh.
Muon go: xoa "Shared Files Local CA" trong phan chung chi cua dien thoai.


3. HAI MAY KHAC MANG (khac wifi, khac nha)
------------------------------------------
Ca hai deu phai mo cung mot dia chi ung dung, nghia la may chay ung dung phai
truy cap duoc tu ben ngoai (dat tren VPS, hoac mo port / dung ngrok).
Sau do: may A dua ma 6 ky tu (hoac QR / link) - may B nhap ma do vao o
"Nhap ma cua nguoi kia" -> Ket noi. File van di thang may sang may.

Neu mang chan ket noi truc tiep (NAT doi xung) thi can them TURN server:
  Windows  : set TURN_URL=turn:host:3478 & set TURN_USERNAME=u & set TURN_PASSWORD=p & node server.js
  Mac/Linux: TURN_URL=turn:host:3478 TURN_USERNAME=u TURN_PASSWORD=p node server.js


4. LINH TINH
------------
Doi cong:
  Windows  : set PORT=8080 & node server.js
  Mac/Linux: PORT=8080 node server.js

Nhan file lon (vai GB) nhanh nhat: bam "Chon thu muc luu" o cuoi trang
(Chrome/Edge tren may tinh) de ghi thang xuong o dia.

File di THANG tu may nay sang may kia, khong luu tren server, duoc ma hoa
dau-cuoi boi WebRTC.

Tat ung dung: bam Ctrl + C trong cua so den, hoac dong cua so.

Go cai dat:
  Windows  : chay Go-cai-dat.bat trong %LOCALAPPDATA%\SharedFiles
  macOS    : chay Go-cai-dat-SharedFiles.command trong thu muc Applications
  Linux    : sh ~/.local/share/shared-files/go-cai-dat.sh
